# Görsel promptları (ChatGPT)

Tüm görseller ChatGPT'nin görsel üretimiyle yapılır. Promptlar İngilizcedir, çünkü stil terimlerini (paper-cut, deckled edge, washi tape) İngilizce daha tutarlı uygular. Açıklamalar Türkçedir. `{SÜSLÜ PARANTEZ}` içindeki yerleri her seferinde doldurun.

## Bir kerelik kurulum

1. ChatGPT'de **"AnneKolay Görsel"** adında bir **Proje** açın.
2. Projeye şu dosyaları yükleyin (Varlıklar sekmesinden indirin): `karakter-karti.png`, `ada.png`, `defne.png`, `pamuk.png`, ayrıca örnek olarak `p01-tanisin.png` ve `p08-gece.png`.
3. Aşağıdaki **Proje talimatını** projenin "Talimatlar" alanına yapıştırın. Bundan sonra bu projede açtığınız her sohbet stili ve karakterleri hatırlar.
4. Proje kullanmıyorsanız, her promptun başına Proje talimatının tamamını ekleyip karakter kartını görsele iliştirin.

> **Altın kural:** Görselin içine Türkçe yazı yazdırmayın; yapay zekâ ğ, ş, ı harflerinde hata yapar. Yazılar Canva'da eklenir. Her promptta "no text" ifadesi bu yüzden vardır.

> **Oran:** ChatGPT dikey görselleri en fazla 2:3 üretir. Gönderiler için "portrait" isteyin ve Canva'da 4:5 tuvale yerleştirip kenarları zemin rengiyle doldurun. Reels kapakları için de "portrait" isteyin ve 9:16 tuvalde ortalayın.

## Proje talimatı

```
You create illustrations for AnneKolay, a Turkish Instagram account for pregnant women and new mothers. Every image must follow this style and these characters exactly. Always use the attached character sheet and character PNGs as the reference for faces, hair, outfits and proportions.

[STYLE]
Handcrafted paper-cut collage illustration. Every shape is cut from matte, slightly textured paper with visible paper fibres, softly irregular hand-cut edges and gentle layered drop shadows (2-4 mm paper depth), like a paper diorama photographed flat from directly above in soft, even daylight. Flat colour on each paper piece: no outlines, no gradients, no gloss, no 3D render, no photorealism, no plastic or clay look. Faces are minimal: closed curved eyes or small dot eyes, a tiny smile, soft rosy cheeks, a tiny nose line at most. Warm, calm, tender, editorial mood with generous empty space.
Palette, strictly only these colours: cream paper #FAF8F5, linen #EFE6D8, sage #8A9A86, light sage #DCE3D8, deep sage #4F5E4B, terracotta #C2785C, peach #F2D3C2, deep clay #8A4A33, bronze #8C6D3B, dark earth brown #5A4426, warm charcoal #2E2A26 (night scenes only), and tiny touches of gold #D4AF37.
Never add text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, signatures or emoji to the image.

[ADA]
Ada, 29, a Turkish woman who is pregnant with her first baby (show a clearly rounded bump). Short wavy dark-brown bob at chin length with a side-swept fringe, light freckles on both cheeks, warm light skin, small gold hoop earrings. Wears a sage-green (#8A9A86) midi dress with a light-sage (#DCE3D8) rounded Peter Pan collar; a slightly lighter sage paper layer highlights her bump, with a tiny peach paper heart on it. Often rests one hand on her bump. Curious, hopeful, a little anxious.

[DEFNE]
Defne, 32, a Turkish woman and mother of a 2-month-old. Chestnut-brown hair in a loose messy top bun with a yellow pencil stuck through it and a few loose strands framing her face, warm medium skin. Wears a terracotta (#C2785C) chunky knit sweater with a darker ribbed crew neck. A cream muslin burp cloth with small sage dots always lies on her right shoulder. Calm, experienced, warm sense of humour, a little tired but smiling.

[PAMUK]
Pamuk, Defne's baby. Swaddled in a cream muslin wrap with two soft peach stripes, wearing a small light-sage knit beanie with a tiny pom-pom, round rosy cheeks, usually sleepy closed eyes. Gender is never shown.

Keep the characters identical in every image: same hair, outfit, signature details and proportions.
```

## 1. Karakter sayfası (bir kez)

Tüm sonraki görsellerin referansı olur. En beğendiğiniz sonucu projeye `karakter-karti-v2.png` olarak yükleyin.

```
Create a character turnaround sheet in the [STYLE] for [ADA], [DEFNE] holding [PAMUK], and [PAMUK] alone. For Ada and Defne show front view, three-quarter view and side view, full body, standing on nothing, evenly spaced on a plain cream paper background. Label nothing. Landscape 3:2.
```

## 2. İfade sayfası (her karakter için bir kez)

```
Create an expression sheet for {ADA / DEFNE} in the [STYLE]: six head-and-shoulders portraits in a 3x2 grid on plain linen paper: 1 peaceful with closed eyes, 2 happy smiling, 3 surprised with small round mouth, 4 tired but smiling, 5 thoughtful looking up, 6 happy tears. Same hair, outfit and details in all six. No text. Landscape 3:2.
```

Background: light sage paper (#DCE3D8). Leave the top 35% of the image as empty background for a headline. Portrait orientation, no text.

## Düzeltme promptları

```
Keep everything exactly the same, change only this: {DEĞİŞİKLİK}.
```

```
The character drifted from the reference. Redraw the same scene but match the attached character sheet exactly: {hair / outfit / signature detail}.
```

```
This looks too smooth or 3D. Redo it as flat cut paper: visible paper fibres, hand-cut irregular edges, layered drop shadows, no gradients, no shine.
```

```
Remove all text, letters and numbers from the image and keep everything else identical.
```
