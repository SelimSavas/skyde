YUP AKADEMİ · TABAĞINDAKİ BİLİM · ANİMASYONA HAZIR GEMINI PROMPTLARI
Gemini tek kare görsel üretir. Bu promptlar o kareleri hareket ettirilebilir parçalar hâlinde istiyor:
katmanlar, kukla parçaları, ağız şekilleri, aşama kareleri, efektler. Sondaki H bölümü Gemini'nin
video özelliği (Veo) için hareket promptlarıdır.

GENEL KURALLAR
- Kesilip ayrılacak her parça düz MACENTA (#FF00FF) zemin üzerinde istenir. Paletimizde macenta yok,
  bu yüzden "arka planı kaldır" araçları (Canva, CapCut, remove.bg) parçaları temiz ayırır. Krem zemin,
  krem pantolon ve buzdolabıyla karışır.
- Aşama karelerini (E bölümü) tek görselde şerit olarak değil, AYNI SOHBETTE art arda düzenleme olarak
  iste: önce 1. kareyi üret, sonra her adımda "keep everything identical, only change ..." de. Böylece
  kadraj sabit kalır ve kareler arasında yumuşak geçiş (crossfade) yapılabilir.
- Karakterli her promptta karakter görselini yükle.
- Stil son ekini hiç değiştirme.

E · AŞAMA KARELERİ (bölüm başına; aynı sohbette art arda)
════════════════════════════════════════
Önce 1. kareyi tam prompt olarak yaz. Sonraki her kare için aynı sohbette şunu yaz:
"Keep everything identical: same framing, same objects, same colors, same lighting. Only change: ..."

E1 · Soğan Neden Ağlatır?
Kare 1: Close-up 16:9 shot of a whole onion on a wooden cutting board with a chef's knife beside it, kitchen softly blurred behind. + STİL SON EKİ
  → the onion is cut in half, showing its rings
  → a faint pale-yellow mist starts rising from the cut surface
  → the mist rises higher and drifts toward the top of the image
  → the mist has spread across the top, small tear drops fall from above at the top edge

E2 · Acı Biber Ağzını Neden Gerçekten "Yakar"?
Kare 1: Close-up 16:9 shot of a whole fresh red chili pepper on a wooden board, kitchen softly blurred behind. + STİL SON EKİ
  → the chili is sliced open lengthwise, showing pale inner ribs and seeds
  → the pale inner ribs glow softly orange
  → a small glass of milk appears next to the chili
  → the orange glow fades as a little milk splashes beside the pepper

E3 · Ekmek Neden Bayatlar?
Kare 1: Close-up 16:9 shot of a slice of fresh soft Turkish bread on a board, fluffy white crumb, golden crust. + STİL SON EKİ
  → the same slice one day later: the crumb a little paler and firmer, crust less shiny
  → three days later: the crumb dry, pale and cracked, crust hard and dull
  → the same old slice warming inside a glowing oven, soft heat waves around it
  → after warming: the crumb looks soft again, a little steam rises (it softens when warm, but goes stale again even faster after cooling)

E4 · Patlamış Mısır Nasıl Patlar?
Kare 1: Large close-up 16:9 cross-section of a single corn kernel on a deep navy background: a hard golden outer shell, a pale starchy inside, and a tiny water droplet in the center. + STİL SON EKİ
  → the kernel glows orange with heat, the water droplet turns into small steam bubbles
  → the shell bulges and small cracks appear
  → the shell bursts open with a star-shaped flash, white foamy starch puffing out
  → a fully popped fluffy white popcorn with pieces of the golden shell attached

E5 · Kızaran Etin O Güzel Kokusu Nereden Gelir?
Kare 1: Top-down 16:9 view of a raw pink steak in a dark cast-iron pan, before cooking. + STİL SON EKİ
  → the edges turn grey, a little steam rises
  → golden brown patches appear, oil sizzles
  → a deep brown crust, golden aroma swirls rising
  → too far: black burnt patches and grey smoke (the "stop here" moment)

H · GEMINI VİDEO (Veo): GÖRSELİ HAREKETLENDİRME
════════════════════════════════════════
Görseli yükle, video seçeneğini aç, aşağıdakinden birini yaz. Klipler kısa (yaklaşık 8 saniye).
Hareketleri küçük tut, yoksa 2D stil bozulur. Üretilen sesi kapat; anlatımı ve müziği biz ekliyoruz.
Video hakkı sınırlı olabilir; en önemli sahnelere sakla.

H1 · Canlı mutfak (arka plan döngüsü)
Static camera. Bring the kitchen gently to life: steam rises slowly from the pot and the teapot, the small blue flames flicker, the oil in the pan sizzles with tiny droplets, dust sparkles float in the sunbeam, the basil leaves sway slightly. Nothing else moves. Keep the flat 2D vector style, colors and every object exactly the same. No people, no text, no speech, no music.

H2 · Sunucu selam veriyor
Static camera. The character smiles, blinks once and waves hello with her free hand, then lifts the magnifying glass to her eye so her eye looks big through the lens. Smooth, simple 2D animation movement. Keep the flat 2D vector style and the character exactly the same. Plain background stays still. No text, no speech, no music.

H3 · Büyüteç geçişi
The camera pushes slowly and smoothly into the magnifying glass lens until the golden glowing lens fills the whole frame, revealing a deep navy microscopic space with soft golden light particles drifting. Keep the flat 2D vector style. No text, no speech, no music.

H4 · Mısır patlıyor (ağır çekim)
Static camera, slow motion. The corn kernels at the bottom of the pot shake, then one by one burst open into fluffy white popcorn that jumps up and falls back softly. Keep the flat 2D vector style and colors. No text, no speech, no music.

H5 · Tava cızırdıyor
Static camera, top-down. The steak sizzles in the pan, tiny oil droplets jump, golden aroma swirls rise and curl upward, the crust slowly deepens in color. Keep the flat 2D vector style and colors. No text, no speech, no music.

H6 · Soğan kesiliyor
Static close-up. The knife slowly cuts through the onion, the two halves fall apart showing the rings, and a faint pale-yellow mist begins rising from the cut surface. Keep the flat 2D vector style and colors. No hands, no people, no text, no speech, no music.
