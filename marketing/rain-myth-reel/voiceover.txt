--- Fact check ---
US National Weather Service: Probability of Precipitation (PoP) = C × A, where C is the forecaster's
confidence that precipitation will occur somewhere in the area and A is the fraction of the area
expected to receive measurable precipitation. Example: 60% confident × 50% of the area = 30%.
