TITLE: 3°C tonight. Are your blossoms safe? 🌸❄️

CAPTION:
3°C on the thermometer doesn't mean your blossoms are safe. 🌸❄️
On clear, calm nights the ground cools faster than the air, so it can freeze down low while the air still reads 3°C.
Watch for 3 signs: clear sky · no wind · 3°C or less in the evening.
April 2025: frost hit 36 provinces in Türkiye.
