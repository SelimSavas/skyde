SKYDE — 7 Instagram Reels · voiceover scripts + post kits (@skyde.app)

HOW TO RECORD
• English, one take per reel. Leave a clear pause (~1 second) between lines; I cut the take at the
  pauses and place every line on its cue, like the farmer and "30% rain" reels.
• The [time] is the window the line has to fit in. Shorter is fine; longer will be retimed.
• Suggested voices: 1–3, 6 → upbeat & clear · 4, 5 → warm & calm · 7 → soft, almost a whisper.

01 · FEELS LIKE — "Why does 10°C feel like 4°C?"            25 s · tone: curious, friendly
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
[00.2 – 03.6]  It says ten degrees. So why does it feel like four?
[04.1 – 10.6]  Two things change the feel. Wind pulls heat off your skin. And when it's hot, humid air stops your sweat from cooling you down.
[11.2 – 15.4]  So dress for the second number — the "feels like" — not the big one.
[15.9 – 20.4]  Or let Skyde just tell you what to wear.
[21.2 – 24.6]  Skyde. Weather, without the weather app feeling.

Caption
It says 10°C. Your body says 4°C. 🥶
Wind steals heat from your skin; humid air stops sweat from cooling you.
Rule of thumb: dress for the "feels like", not the big number.
Follow for weather that actually makes sense ☁️

[16.9 – 19.6]  One night can cost a whole season.

3 signs: clear sky · no wind · 3°C or less in the evening.
April 2025: frost hit 36 provinces in Türkiye.
